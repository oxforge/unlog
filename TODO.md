| anchor-yearless-syslog | **Today:** RFC 3164 syslog lines carry no year (`Jan 15 10:29:55 db-01 postgres[1234]: ...`). `ingest/parser_syslog.go` takes the year from a reference date: the file's mtime (the archive entry's or gzip header's time, or now for stdin), stepping back a year when the result would be more than a day after it.
